At **Giesecke+Devrient (G+D)** in Barcelona, Spain, Andrea served as Full Stack Engineer within the R&D division, engineering secure mobility prototypes, high-throughput enterprise telecom microservices, and automated testing frameworks.

---

## 1. Biometric Android Vehicle HMI (MWC 2019 Showcase)

- **Biometric In-Vehicle Authentication**: Led development of a native Android Human-Machine Interface (HMI) integrating hardware-level biometric authentication (fingerprint and facial recognition) into simulated automotive instrument clusters.
- **Live Exhibition Delivery**: Successfully presented and operated the live prototype at the **Mobile World Congress (MWC 2019)** in Barcelona, showcasing secure digital car key handoffs and driver profile personalization.

---

## 2. Telecom Enterprise Microservices

- **High-Volume Notification Services**: Architected resilient microservices in **Java and Spring Boot** responsible for orchestrating real-time, high-volume transactional messaging for major telecommunications carriers.
- **Fault-Tolerant Delivery Channels**: Implemented message queueing, exponential backoff retries, and circuit breaker patterns to guarantee carrier-grade SLA compliance.

---

## 3. Parameterized BDD Test Automation Engine

- **Custom BDD Framework**: Designed a scalable test automation harness combining **Cucumber**, **Selenium**, and Java, integrating with internal test registries and continuous integration pipelines.
- **Regression Elimination**: Automated hundreds of integration workflows, cutting manual QA testing cycles from days to automated minutes prior to client delivery.
